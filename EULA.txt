2. THE KAYBORO VIRTUAL SCREEN DRIVER
Kayboro installs a small display driver called the Kayboro Virtual Screen Driver the first time you install
it. On Steam this happens once, during first-time setup, and Windows asks for administrator approval.

What it is for: the "Hide Game Window" feature. Kayboro uses the driver to create an invisible screen, moves
your game onto it, and shows the game inside a shell in Kayboro. Your game keeps running at full speed, and
you don't see a second copy of it on your desktop.

What it does:
  - Adds a display adapter named "Kayboro Virtual Screen" to Windows.
  - Creates invisible screens only when you use Hide Game Window, at the size of your shell.
  - Removes those screens automatically within a few seconds when Kayboro closes, so a game can never be
    left on a screen you can't see.

What it does not do:
  - It does not record, store or send anything, and it has no network access.
  - It does not change your other monitors or their settings.
  - It does nothing at all while Kayboro is not running.

Removing it: uninstalling Kayboro removes the driver. You can also remove it at any time:
  - Device Manager > Display adapters > Kayboro Virtual Screen > Uninstall device (tick "Attempt to remove
    the driver for this device"), or
  - run "driver\kbvdd-setup.exe uninstall" from the Kayboro folder as an administrator.
Kayboro works without the driver; only Hide Game Window needs it.

The driver is open source under the Microsoft Public License and is based on Microsoft's Indirect Display
Driver sample.
