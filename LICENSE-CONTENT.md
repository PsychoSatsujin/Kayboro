# Shellcade Content License

This file covers the art and media shipped with Shellcade: shells (frames), wallpapers, fills, colorways and finishes under `src/renderer/` and `src/catalog/`.

## Shellcade originals

Shells and wallpapers with `"author": "Shellcade"` are original works of the Shellcade project. You may use them inside Shellcade and show them in screenshots, videos and streams. Do not redistribute or sell them separately.

**DLC shells** are Shellcade originals or licensed designs sold as Steam DLC. They are licensed to the Steam account that owns the DLC.

## Third-party and fan content

Wallpapers and items made by others keep their creator's rights and are credited in the catalog (`credit: {artist, source, url}`). They are unlocked with in-app coins or free, never sold for money, and are removed on a takedown request from anyone in the chain. See [docs/CONTENT-POLICY.md](docs/CONTENT-POLICY.md).

## Your local content

Wallpaper Engine subscriptions, local images, GIFs and videos are read from your own disk and never uploaded or redistributed by Shellcade.

## Mods

Mods belong to their authors. Shellcade loads them as-is and does not redistribute them.
